**Description**

Hit. Stand. Then twist the table.

Twist 21 takes the blackjack idea you already know — get close to 21 without going over — and adds action cards that change the round. Play against two rivals, Ava and Rex. First to 5 points wins the match.

HOW A TURN WORKS
- Play one action card if you want
- Then Hit or Stand
- Your first card stays face-down to everyone else

ACTION CARDS
Skip, Peek, Trim, Shield, and Reverse are free. Premium adds Swap, Force Hit, and two cards that move the target itself: Target 18 busts everyone above 18, and Target 24 gives everyone room.

- 5 action cards
