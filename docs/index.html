Twist 21

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Twist 21 is a card game rated 17+, published by Samay Vashsht. Blackjack with action cards: hit and stand against two rivals, then play Skip, Swap, Peek, Shield and more to bend the table, even the target number itself. Pick a table with a boot of 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 chips, and the match winner takes the pot. Free practice with no boot is always available.

Chips are free virtual game currency. You start with free chips, can claim a daily bonus, and can spend chips on table boots, the odds meter and cosmetic looks. Optional chip packs can be bought. Chips have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes. Twist 21 is not real-money gambling and offers no real-money prizes.

Chip packs are optional consumable in-app purchases bought through Apple In-App Purchase: 12,000 chips (US$0.99), 50,000 (US$2.99), 100,000 (US$4.99), 250,000 (US$9.99) and 600,000 (US$19.99). They are one-time purchases, not subscriptions, and they do not renew. There is no free trial. Before you confirm, the purchase screen shows the exact price Apple will charge in your currency.

All action cards and unlimited matches are free. There is no account and no ads. Purchases are handled by Apple, with RevenueCat helping to process and restore them. See the Privacy Policy for details.
