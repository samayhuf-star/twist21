Twist 21

Blackjack with action cards. Hit and stand against two rivals, then play Skip, Swap, Peek, Shield and more to bend the table, and even the target number itself.

Free to play: 3 matches a day and 5 action cards. Twist 21 Premium is a one-time purchase (US $39.99, charged by Apple at the price shown before you confirm). It unlocks all 9 action cards, unlimited matches, and the bust-chance meter. No subscription and no ads.

Twist 21 has no betting, no chips, and no real-money play. No account is required.
