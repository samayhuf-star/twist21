The game

Twist 21 is a solo card game. You play against two opponents that run on your device. There is no account and no login. Free play includes 3 matches a day and 5 action cards (Skip, Peek, Trim, Shield, and Reverse).

Twist 21 Premium

Premium is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase, product ID twist21_lifetime. It unlocks:

  • Swap, Force Hit, Target 18, and Target 24
  • Unlimited matches (the 3-a-day limit is removed)
  • The bust-chance meter on your turn

Premium is not a subscription. It does not renew, and there is no free trial. Before you confirm, the purchase screen shows the full amount Apple will charge. In the United States that amount is $39.99. Apple bills your store account. To restore a purchase, open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same store account.
