The game

Twist 21 is a solo card game against two opponents that run on your device. There is no account or login. All action cards and unlimited matches are free. The first player to 5 points wins the match.

Tables, boots and chips

Chips are free virtual game currency. You start with free chips, can claim a daily bonus, and can spend chips on table boots, the odds meter and cosmetic looks. Optional chip packs can be bought. Chips have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes. Twist 21 is not real-money gambling and offers no real-money prizes. Choose a boot of 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 chips from the Table menu. Each player puts in the boot and the match winner takes the pot. Free practice with no boot is always available.

Chip packs are optional consumable in-app purchases bought through Apple In-App Purchase: 12,000 chips (US$0.99), 50,000 (US$2.99), 100,000 (US$4.99), 250,000 (US$9.99) and 600,000 (US$19.99).
