The game

Twist 21 is a solo card game. You play against two opponents that run on your device. There is no account and no login. The game is free to play: every action card and unlimited matches are included.

Chips

Chips are an in-game item with no cash value. You start with 10,000 free chips, can claim a daily bonus, and earn more by winning rounds and matches. Chips are never staked on the result of a match. You can spend them on extra action cards (Double, Freeze, Steal), card backs, table themes, and the bust-odds meter.

If you want more chips you can buy a pack as a consumable in-app purchase: 5,000 chips, 18,000, 35,000, 80,000, or 200,000. Packs are not subscriptions and do not renew. The purchase screen shows the full price Apple will charge before you confirm. Chips cannot be refunded by the app or exchanged for money; refund requests go to Apple. Consumable purchases cannot be restored on a new device, so keep your chips by playing on one device.

Acceptable use

The game has no betting and no real-money play. Chips cannot be cashed out. Do not use the app to harass anyone or to break the law.
