The short version

Twist 21 is a card game you play on your device. It has no accounts, no advertising and no betting or real-money play. There is no analytics SDK.

What stays on your device

Game progress such as how many free matches you have played today is stored on your device (including in the game view’s local storage).

Purchases

Chips Pass is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly) and chip packs are consumable in-app purchases, sold by the Apple App Store or Google Play. The price is shown in the app before you buy. Chips are virtual game currency with no cash value. We never see your payment card details.

Purchases are checked on your device with the store's own signed transactions. No purchase data is sent to us or to any third-party service. Your chip balance and Chips Pass status are stored only on your device.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale of data, and no collection of contact lists, location or photos.

Children

Twist 21 is a card game with no betting or real-money play and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
