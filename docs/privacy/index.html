Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 November 2026

Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht (samayhuf@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with information. The app has no account, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your match progress, chip balance, table choice, daily bonus status, cosmetic looks and stats are stored on your device in the app's local storage. We run no game server and cannot see this data. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases and RevenueCat

Chip packs are optional consumable in-app purchases bought through Apple In-App Purchase: 12,000 chips (US$0.99), 50,000 (US$2.99), 100,000 (US$4.99), 250,000 (US$9.99) and 600,000 (US$19.99). They are one-time purchases, not subscriptions, and they do not renew. There is no free trial. Before you confirm, the purchase screen shows the exact price Apple will charge in your currency. Apple processes the payment, and we never receive your name, email or payment card number. To deliver and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat (revenuecat.com), a purchase service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase and receipt information. This is not linked to your identity and is used only for App Functionality (delivering chips and restoring purchases). It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not collect

  • No name, email or account, unless you email us yourself.
  • No advertising and no analytics or tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No sale of data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone or health information.

Chips

Chips are free virtual game currency. You start with free chips, can claim a daily bonus, and can spend chips on table boots, the odds meter and cosmetic looks. Optional chip packs can be bought. Chips have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes. Twist 21 is not real-money gambling and offers no real-money prizes.

How long it is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app or clear its storage. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records so a restore can succeed on a new install. We keep no separate copy.

Your choices

You can play free without buying, decline any purchase sheet, and use free practice with no boot. There is no account to delete in the app. Questions or deletion requests about purchase records: email samayhuf@gmail.com.

Children

Twist 21 is rated 17+ because it is a blackjack-style card game with virtual chips. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Contact: Samay Vashsht, samayhuf@gmail.com.
