Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht (samayhuf@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with information. It collects almost nothing, and it does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your match progress, how many free matches you have played today, and a local note of whether Premium is unlocked are stored on your device (in the app's local storage). We do not run a game server and we cannot see that data. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Twist 21 Premium is a one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription and it does not renew. The amount Apple charges is the price shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. In the United States that price is $39.99. Apple processes the payment. We never receive your name, email, or payment card number.

We use RevenueCat to confirm the purchase and to restore it on this device. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID generated on the device, the store product you bought, and whether that purchase is active. RevenueCat uses this only so the app can unlock Premium and restore it. Their policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

If you installed Twist 21 from an Apple Search Ads campaign, the app may send Apple's install attribution token to RevenueCat. That token is not used to advertise to you or to build a profile.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, or email, unless you email us yourself.
  • No advertising, and no analytics trackers.
  • No sale of data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health information.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

How long it is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app or clear its storage. RevenueCat keeps the anonymous purchase record so a later restore can succeed. We do not keep a separate copy.

Your choices

You can play the free game without buying. You can decline the purchase sheet. To ask RevenueCat to delete the anonymous app user ID associated with this install, email samayhuf@gmail.com. There is no account to delete inside the app, because the app does not create one.

Children

Twist 21 is rated 17+ because it is a blackjack-style card game. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: Samay Vashsht, samayhuf@gmail.com.
