Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht (samayhuf@gmail.com). This policy explains what the app does with information. It collects almost nothing, and it does not require an account.

What stays on your device

Your match progress, your chip balance, daily bonus streak, stats and cosmetics are stored on your device (in the app's local storage). We do not run a game server and we cannot see that data. Deleting the app removes it.

Purchases

Chip packs are optional one-time (consumable) in-app purchases. They are not subscriptions. Apple processes the payment and we never receive your name, email, or payment card number.

We use RevenueCat to handle purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt, and basic device and app information, so it can confirm the purchase and add the chips. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it is not linked to your identity. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No account, name, or email, unless you email us yourself.
  • No advertising, and no analytics trackers.
  • No sale of data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or health information.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

How long it is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies. We do not keep a separate copy.

Your choices

You can play the free game without buying. You can decline the purchase sheet. Deleting the app removes your on-device chips and stats. There is no account to delete, because the app does not create one. Questions: samayhuf@gmail.com.

Children

Twist 21 is rated 17+ because it is a blackjack-style card game. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: Samay Vashsht, samayhuf@gmail.com.
