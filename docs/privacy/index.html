Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it collects almost nothing.

What stays on your device

Your game progress, the count of free matches you have played today, your chip balance and your Chips Pass status are stored on your device. We do not run accounts and we cannot see this data.

Purchases

Chips Pass (an auto-renewing subscription) and chip packs (consumable) are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Purchases are checked on your device with the store's signed transactions; no purchase data is sent to us or to any third-party service. Chips are virtual game currency with no cash value. We do not receive your name, payment card or email from the store.

What we do not do

No advertising, no analytics trackers, no sale of data, and no collection of contact lists, location or photos.

Children

Twist 21 is a card game with no betting or real-money play and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: samayhuf@gmail.com.
