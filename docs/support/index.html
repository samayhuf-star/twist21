How to play

  • Tap Deal me in. You play against Ava and Rex. First to 5 points wins the match.
  • On your turn you may play one action card, then Hit or Stand.
  • Your first card stays face-down to the others. The target starts at 21.
  • Rules, Terms of Use and Privacy Policy are linked from the menu.

Tables and chips

Open Menu, then Table, to pick a boot of 100, 1,000, 10,000 or 100,000 chips; the match winner takes the pot. Choose Free practice for no boot. If you lack the chips for a table, the app switches you to free practice. Chips are free virtual game currency. You start with free chips, can claim a daily bonus, and can spend chips on table boots, the odds meter and cosmetic looks. Optional chip packs can be bought. Chips have no cash value. They cannot be withdrawn, cashed out, sold, transferred, or exchanged for money, goods, or prizes. Twist 21 is not real-money gambling and offers no real-money prizes.

Buying chips and restoring

Chip packs are optional consumable in-app purchases bought through Apple In-App Purchase: 12,000 chips (US$0.99), 50,000 (US$2.99), 100,000 (US$4.99), 250,000 (US$9.99) and 600,000 (US$19.99). They are one-time purchases, not subscriptions, and they do not renew. There is no free trial. Before you confirm, the purchase screen shows the exact price Apple will charge in your currency. To restore, open the chip shop (tap your chip balance) and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple account. Restoring does not charge you again.
