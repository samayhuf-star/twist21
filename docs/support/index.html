Support

Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht. Email samayhuf@gmail.com and include your device model and what happened. There is no account and no login.

How to play

  • Tap Deal me in. You play against Ava and Rex. First to 5 points wins the match.
  • On your turn you may play one action card, then Hit or Stand.
  • Your first card stays face-down to the others. Number cards run from 1 to 10. The target starts at 21.
  • Rules stay available from the Rules button, including the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and this Support page.

Free play and Premium

Free play is 3 matches a day and 5 action cards. The daily count resets at midnight on your device. Twist 21 Premium is a one-time purchase (US $39.99, shown in your local price before you pay). It unlocks Swap, Force Hit, Target 18, Target 24, unlimited matches, and the bust-chance meter. It is not a subscription.

Restore Premium

Open Premium in the app (the Go Premium button) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple account you used to buy. You do not need to pay again.
