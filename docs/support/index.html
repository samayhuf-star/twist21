Twist 21 is made by Samay Vashsht. Email samayhuf@gmail.com and include your device model and what happened. There is no account and no login.

How to play

  • Tap Deal me in. You play against Ava and Rex. First to 5 points wins the match.
  • On your turn you may play one action card, then Hit or Stand.
  • Your first card stays face-down to the others. Number cards run from 1 to 10. The target starts at 21.
  • Rules stay available from the Rules button, including the Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and this Support page.

Free play and chips

Twist 21 is free to play: all action cards and unlimited matches. You start with 10,000 chips, get a daily bonus, and earn chips by winning. Chips buy extra cards, card backs, table themes and the bust-odds meter. Extra chips are optional consumable purchases (5,000 to 200,000 chips). Chips have no cash value and are never staked on a match.

Chips and purchases

If a purchase went through but chips did not arrive, reopen the app and they are added automatically. If they still are not there, email us with the date of the purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
