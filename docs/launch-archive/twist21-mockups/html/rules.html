Rules

How to play

Blackjack with a twist

Finish closest to the target — it starts at 21 — without going over. Number cards run 1 to 10. First to 5 points wins the match.

01

Action

Play one action if you want. Start with one. Limit 3.

02

Hit or Stand

Hit to draw, or Stand to lock. An action draw ends the turn.

03

Hole card

Your first card is hidden from others. Peek can reveal one.

Win

1 or 2 points

Closest scores 1. Exact target scores 2.

Tie

No points

A shared best total scores nothing.
